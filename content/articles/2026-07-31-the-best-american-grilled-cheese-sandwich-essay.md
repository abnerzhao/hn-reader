## easy

Grilled cheese is a sandwich with bread and melted cheese. You can add onion, avocado, bacon, orsanitize other toppings. Cook it in a pan, on a flame, or in a broiler.

The sandwich is warm, soft, and creamy cheese melts inside. It brings simple joy and can even make people cry. The sandwich is small but perfect, a small, sweet comfort.

Make it with any bread or cheese you like. It can have carrots, onions, or a spicy mayo. Enjoy the simple warmth and feel peaceful joy.

Grilled cheese is a small, bright moment in any day.

## medium

In writing about the grilled cheese sandwich, I find myself with little to offer but praise. It is difficult to encounter such a thing and not be overcome by gladness. This is not a space for complaining about air-fryer methods, gourmet additions, or the proper application of butter. To me, each grilled cheese is enough, and more than enough. In a world full of slaughter and fire, where doubt and monstrosity abound, the grilled cheese remains a small and perfect thing.

My position on the grilled cheese is unabashedly boosterish and agnostic toward ingredients and provenance. You may add caramelized onions, avocado, bacon, or ham; chutney or cornichons. You may use Wonderbread or a freshly-baked eighteen-grain country loaf. You can use goat cheese, smoked gouda, or even unidentified processed cheese, heated in a pan, under a broiler, or with an acetylene torch. I do not care. You have composed something perfect with your own hands, a moment of creation that grants the human animal a sliver of divinity.

From two perfect things—bread and cheese—arises a more perfect union. Each grilled cheese sandwich is entirely itself. The simplicity of the sandwich engenders its own kind of awe: an internal pause at the perception of this unalloyed good. You can be too poor or too overwhelmed to obtain one, but the thought of it is attainable to anyone; the ideal is there, pleasant to a mind parched of gladness.

This thing is humble, not hallowed; it provides not ecstasy, but satisfaction, which is easier to obtain and less giddy to receive. You can hold it in the palm of your hand and be content. Each grilled cheese sandwich is a reflection of all other grilled cheese sandwiches that have come before it, part of a great whole that rings the world in fat and starchy warmth. It is easy to get caught up in the innumerable recipes, but it is better to regard the sandwich as an ideal, a thing-in-itself.

The grilled cheese is an object of consistent joy, a reliable and ordinary pleasure that is as common as light and as necessary. While life cannot be all simple pleasures, we must hope for their return, as light returns. I hope you find your small perfect thing, and in doing so, welcome joy’s arrival. Go to your kitchen, find a loaf of bread, add cheese, apply heat, and attain a moment of satisfaction to which you may return at any time.

## hard

In the Notable Sandwiches column every travel takes the form of a story told on the wings of bread, and this week the author returns to a comfort that has survived the test of time. The grilled cheese sandwich, a humble fusion of fat and starch, earns its place at the very top of the alphabetized list and its own single entry. The piece is written in the same earnest style that turns an ordinary bite into a small, triumphant moment.

Padma Lakshmi, whose name carries the weight of culinary fame, admits she had never expected an essay about a grilled cheese to bring tears to her eyes. She notes that Talia Lavin’s treat of the sandwich, written with the religious fervor of an initiate and yet so openly sincere, feels almost like a benediction of the small and perfect things. The writer gives the sandwich a blessing.

The speaker acknowledges the loneliness that is part of being a writer, maintaining that the reverberating applause of readers makes whatever weariness come from selling oneself or touring cities a bit lighter. The intimacy of the moment when a grilled cheese is prepared, shared, and devoured is an act that whispers of human divinity, a tiny slice of grace that prevents the world from slipping into endless conflict.
